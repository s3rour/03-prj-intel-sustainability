Sustainability Through the Ages

Explore Intel's journey through time, discovering how our commitment to innovation has shaped a more sustainable future for technology and our planet.

Scroll to view timeline | Hover over cards to learn more

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore rename NM Electronics to Intel Corporation, laying the foundation for decades of technological innovation.

1968 — Intel Founded

Founded by Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore, Intel set out to build semiconductor memory products — sparking decades of breakthroughs and a company-wide commitment to responsible manufacturing.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel debuts the 4004, the world's first commercial microprocessor, igniting the microprocessor revolution and propelling the future of computing.

1971 — First Microprocessor

The 4004 packed 2,300 transistors onto a chip the size of a fingernail — setting the trajectory for the miniaturization that makes today's energy-efficient chips possible.

1978

8086 Processor

Launch of the 8086 processor, establishing the x86 architecture that drives countless PCs and servers in the modern era.

1978 — 8086 Processor

The 8086's x86 instruction set became the backbone of modern PCs and servers. Efficiency improvements in x86 chips have compounding effects on global energy consumption to this day.

1985

386 Processor

Intel introduces the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture, ushering in a new era of performance and multitasking for personal computers.

1985 — 386 Processor

32-bit processing unlocked true multitasking. More performance per watt became a guiding principle carried into every subsequent generation of chip design.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

This year marks Intel's highest annual greenhouse gas emissions for operations. Intel begins investing heavily in abatement and renewables to reverse the trend.

2006 — Peak GHG Emissions

Recognizing 2006 as a turning point, Intel launched aggressive investments in chemical abatement, on-site renewable energy, and cleaner manufacturing — beginning a decade-long downward emissions trend.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE strategy and 2030 goals, driving industry-wide progress on climate action, water stewardship, and waste reduction.

2020 — RISE Strategy

RISE: Responsible, Inclusive, Sustainable, Enabling. Goals include net-positive water use, zero total waste to landfill, and conserving more energy than operations consume.

2022

Net-Zero By 2040

Intel announces its commitment to achieve net-zero greenhouse gas emissions (Scope 1 and 2) across global operations by 2040.

2022 — Net-Zero By 2040

The pledge covers Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions across all global facilities. Intel also committed to a 10× improvement in energy efficiency of client and server platforms by 2030.

2023

Renewable Electricity

The company achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide, helping to drastically lower carbon emissions and driving progress toward Intel's long-term sustainability goals.

2023 — Renewable Electricity

Intel sourced clean power through direct renewable energy contracts, green tariffs, and renewable energy certificates across manufacturing sites globally.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit, uniting suppliers, government officials, and industry leaders to collaborate on next-generation sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.

2024 — Sustainability Summit

Hundreds of suppliers and policymakers aligned on shared emissions-reduction targets, responsible mineral sourcing, and circular-economy practices for semiconductor equipment.
